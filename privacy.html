Privacy Policy

PricePro OS · Effective November 6, 2026

PricePro OS is an internal tool operated by PricePro Auto Group (Price Pro in Maumee, Ohio and PricePro Ford in Whitehouse, Ohio). Only our own staff sign in to it. This policy explains what information the app handles, including information it receives from Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

What the app does

  • Publishes posts that our staff approve to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • Reads the performance of those posts (for example reach, views, likes, comments and shares) so our team can see what works.
  • Creates, starts, pauses and reports on ads for our own Pages on our own Meta ad account, only when an authorized manager approves them.

Information we receive from Meta

  • Our Pages' and Instagram accounts' own posts, captions and post IDs.
  • Aggregated performance numbers for those posts and for our ads, as Meta reports them.
  • The settings of our own ad campaigns (budgets, dates, locations and the ads themselves).

The app does not collect the names, profiles, messages or contact details of people who see or interact with our posts or ads. The numbers it reads are totals Meta provides.

Information from our staff

Staff sign in with their PricePro Google Workspace account. The app keeps each staff member's name, work email and role so it can show who made or approved each post or ad.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to run PricePro Auto Group's own marketing. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside PricePro Auto Group except the service providers that host the app and its database, who process it on our behalf.

Storage and security

Information is stored in the app's private database and file storage. Access is limited to signed-in PricePro staff according to their role.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information while it is useful for our marketing records. To ask that information received from Meta be deleted, email hhage@pricepro.co with the subject "PricePro OS data deletion" and say what you would like removed. We will confirm once it is deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hhage@pricepro.co.
